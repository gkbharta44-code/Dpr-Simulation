The tokenomics engine

The monetary framework centers on the ERC-20 Delper Token, owned 100% by GK Bharta. The document describes three core rules.

100% fiat-backed reserve

Tokens are not minted without backing. Each token in circulation is described as backed 1:1 by USD, EUR, or INR held in GK Bharta’s tier-1 banking clearing treasury.

2% transactional tax layer

A 1% tax applies on fiat deposit (buy) and a 1% tax on fiat redemption (sell). The document states that all internal economic activity, from industrial procurement to household groceries, would be transacted in tokens.

6-month liquidity velocity cap

Stakeholders may hold tokens only up to a value equal to six months of their transaction throughput, intended to limit hoarding and control velocity.

Points to verify before relying on this

  • Guarantee and endorsements. The claimed AAA supranational guarantee from the World Bank and AIIB, and any onboarding of sovereign customs agencies, need independent confirmation.
  • Mandatory use and holding caps. Requiring all internal transactions in a single token, with caps on holdings, raises significant legal and regulatory questions in most jurisdictions, including money transmission, securities, and capital controls.
  • Unstated assumptions. The totals reconcile arithmetically (for example, $46.59T ÷ $8.65T ≈ 5.4 years), but the volume, adoption, and tax-capture figures rest on assumptions the document does not explain.
  • Inflation claim. Controlling prices across a closed platform does not by itself remove external inflation, which depends on broader monetary and supply conditions.
